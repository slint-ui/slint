## Clause 5: Distributed Developments

[Distributed Development](/safety-plan/development-process/#distributed-development) explains why the clause reaches only the interface between SixtyFPS GmbH and the integrator.

| Requirement | Topic | Status | How |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5.4.1 | Application of requirements | Met | [Distributed Development](/safety-plan/development-process/#distributed-development). |
| 5.4.2 | Supplier selection criteria | Integrator | The integrator selects Slint SC as a supplier. |
| 5.4.3 | Development interface agreement | Open | [S-02](/safety-plan/lifecycle/#once-to-set-up) plans the agreement template, which doesn't exist yet. |
| 5.4.4 | Execution of distributed development | Partly met | The [User Manual](/user-manual/) states what the integrator needs, and [F-02](/safety-plan/release/#after-a-release) reports safety anomalies. The agreement of 5.4.3 is missing. |
| 5.4.5 | Functional safety assessment | Met | TÜV Nord assesses Slint SC; see [Confirmation Measures](/safety-plan/safety-management/#confirmation-measures). |
| 5.4.6 | Agreement for production and operation | Partly met | [After a Release](/safety-plan/release/#after-a-release). The reporting channel is missing. |
