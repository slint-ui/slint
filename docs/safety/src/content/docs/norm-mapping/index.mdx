The Norm Mapping lists the requirements of ISO 26262:2018 that apply to Slint SC.
For each requirement, it states whether Slint SC meets the requirement, and links to the part of this manual that does.
The rest of the manual describes what we do, and leaves the clause numbers to this mapping.

## Pages

* [ISO 26262-2](/norm-mapping/iso-26262-2/): management of functional safety, Clauses 5 to 7.
* [ISO 26262-6](/norm-mapping/iso-26262-6/): product development at the software level, Clauses 5 to 11 and Annex C.
* [ISO 26262-6 Method Tables](/norm-mapping/iso-26262-6-methods/): the methods of Tables 1 to 15, at ASIL B.
* [ISO 26262-8](/norm-mapping/iso-26262-8/): supporting processes, Clauses 5 to 16.

The other parts apply to Slint SC only partly, or not at all.
[Parts of the Lifecycle](/safety-plan/standards-compliance/#parts-of-the-lifecycle) states which clauses of Parts 3, 4, 5, 7, and 9 apply, and why.

## Status

Each requirement has one of these statuses:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Met | The linked part of the manual meets the requirement. |
| Partly met | The linked part meets the requirement in part. The row names what's missing. |
| Open | Nothing meets the requirement yet. |
| Integrator | The integrator meets the requirement, under the linked assumption of use. |
| N/A | The requirement doesn't apply to Slint SC. The row gives the reason. |

The method tables use Applied, Partly applied, and Not applied instead, since ISO 26262 asks for a combination of methods rather than every method.
A method recommended at ASIL B that isn't applied has its rationale in the row.
