# Assets

The photos, album art, and weather images originate in [Slint's Home Automation demo](../../home-automation/ui/images).
They were copied unchanged through the P4 demo in `slint-ui/ux-playground` and retain their MIT licensing.

Inter is licensed under the SIL Open Font License.
The font's adjacent `.license` file preserves its attribution.

The SVG icons preserve the MIT-licensed paths in the P4 demo's `rust/demo_assets.rs` at revision `ddfe88c`.
They use a 1.65-unit stroke on a 24-unit view box and render as vectors at their requested size.
