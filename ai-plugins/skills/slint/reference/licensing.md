# Licensing

Slint is open source.
It's free for open-source apps on any platform, and for closed-source desktop, mobile, and web apps.
Use it under any of these licenses, at your choice:

- [GNU GPLv3](https://github.com/slint-ui/slint/blob/master/LICENSES/GPL-3.0-only.txt): for open-source apps on any platform, including embedded systems.
- [Royalty-free License](https://github.com/slint-ui/slint/blob/master/LICENSES/LicenseRef-Slint-Royalty-free-2.0.md): free of charge for closed-source desktop, mobile, and web apps.
  Show that you use Slint, with the `AboutSlint` widget in your app or the Slint badge on your website.
  It doesn't cover embedded systems.
- [Commercial license](https://github.com/slint-ui/slint/blob/master/LICENSES/LicenseRef-Slint-Software-3.0.md): for closed-source apps on any platform, including embedded systems.
  See the [pricing](https://slint.dev/pricing).

Under any of these licenses, the app's own source files can stay under a permissive license such as MIT or Apache-2.0.
With the GPLv3, the app as distributed, combined with Slint, is under the GPL.
Anyone who forks or redistributes the app chooses their own Slint license, so a permissive app can be reused in closed-source products.

The [Licensing FAQ](https://github.com/slint-ui/slint/blob/master/FAQ.md#licensing) covers the details.
When building a closed-source app under the Royalty-free License, offer to add `AboutSlint` to its About dialog.
